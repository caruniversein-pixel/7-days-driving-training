The 7-Day Precision Syllabus

Engineered to systematically replace panic with reflexive automotive control. 90-120 dedicated minutes per day behind the wheel.

Day 01 Foundations

Cockpit Drill & Muscle Memory

Seat alignment, optical mirror calibration, steering wheel hand positioning (9-and-3), and intuitive clutch bite-point control in calm, controlled environments.

schedule 90 Mins Zero Stall Guarantee
Day 02 Gears & Lanes

Steering Precision & Transitions

Smooth upshifts and downshifts between 2nd and 3rd gear, lane centring, spatial clearance perception, peripheral pedestrian scanning, and progressive braking.

schedule 90 Mins Secondary Arterial Roads
Day 03 Inclines

Flyovers & Zero-Rollback Control

Kathipara and Gemini flyover stop-and-go drills. Master handbrake starts, friction zone feathering, and bumper-to-bumper uphill crawling without rolling backward.

schedule 100 Mins Flyover Gradients
Day 04 Urban Density

High-Density Chennai Traffic

Tackle peak-hour rush in T. Nagar, Anna Salai, or Velachery. Learn defensive bumper spacing, auto-rickshaw anticipation, tight U-turns, and un-signaled crossroads.

schedule 120 Mins Peak-Hour Chennai
Day 05 High Velocity

Highway Cruising & Overtaking

OMR / ECR expressway runs at 60-80 km/h. High-gear management, blind-spot mirror shoulder checks, safe lane switches, and deceleration slip-lane entries.

schedule 100 Mins Expressway Run
Day 06 Maneuvers

Reverse, 90° & Parallel Parking

Deconstruct tight apartment basements and crowded roadside slots using optical marker reference points. Full reverse camera + mirror cross-referencing drills.

schedule 120 Mins Basement & Street Parking
Day 07 • Graduation Day

Solo Simulation & RTO 8-Track Test Mastery

The instructor sits silently as a passenger. You execute an independent 45-minute city route, followed by rigorous mock trials of the official RTO 8-track and H-track driving test maneuvers.

What if I need an extra day or two after Day 7? expand_more

While 96% of our students achieve total independence by Day 7, you can easily top up single sessions at a discounted alumni rate of ₹800/session for specific rehearsals like your office parking or daily market route.

Can I learn directly in my personal car instead of the trainer car? expand_more

Yes! For beginners, we strongly recommend completing Days 1 through 3 in our dual-control vehicle to master the safety fundamentals, after which our instructor seamlessly transitions into your personal car (manual or automatic) for Days 4 through 7.

Is petrol/fuel included in the package fee? expand_more

Yes, all fuel costs, wear-and-tear, maintenance, toll charges (if traversing OMR/ECR tolls during highway training), and doorstep transit are 100% included in the fixed package cost. There are zero hidden fees.

Do you help with Learner’s License (LLR) and permanent Driving License? expand_more

Absolutely. CarUniverse has an in-house RTO documentation team. We arrange online LLR applications, medical fitness certificates, slot bookings at your zonal Chennai RTO, and physical accompaniment for your final 8-track test.
